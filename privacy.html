Reels Inbox: Privacy Policy

Reels Inbox is a personal, non-commercial tool run by Caleb Peterson. It is used only with Instagram accounts he owns.

What it accesses: direct messages sent to the connected Instagram account (jarvis_is_listening), mainly links to shared reels and short text labels.

How it is used: shared reels are summarized into private notes for the owner. Data is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deletion: see data deletion.
